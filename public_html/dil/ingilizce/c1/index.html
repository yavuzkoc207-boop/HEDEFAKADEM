İngilizce C1 derslerinden örnekler

Her ünite; konuşma, diyalog, dinleme, kelime, cümle veya gramer görevlerini birbiriyle bağlantılı biçimde ilerletir. Aşağıdaki başlıklar platformdaki mevcut ders rotasından örneklerdir.

ÜNİTE 01

Akademik Akıl Yürütme

  • Although this view is understandable, its main assumption is debatable.Diyalog · Isınma
  • The findings indicate a significant relationship between the two variables.Cümle · Gerçek Hayat
  • This decision is only the tip of the iceberg.Kelime · Hız Turu
  • Although the data does not say so explicitly, the trend appears to have changed.Dinleme · Konuşma Görevi
ÜNİTE 02

Nüans ve Sentez

  • Allocating resources now will prevent greater costs later.Konuşma · Isınma
  • More data is needed before reaching a definitive conclusion.Gramer · Gerçek Hayat
  • Although the two approaches appear different, they focus on the same underlying problem.Cümle · Hız Turu
  • This change may affect not only cost but also user behavior.Diyalog · Konuşma Görevi
ÜNİTE 03

Profesyonel Derinlik

  • The opposing view raises an important point; however, it does not prove causation.Diyalog · Isınma
  • The author’s praise becomes clear irony within the context.Dinleme · Gerçek Hayat
  • A compromise that preserves both sides’ main concerns appears possible.Konuşma · Hız Turu
  • In summary, the solution is feasible, but the governance mechanism should be strengthened.Final · Konuşma Görevi
